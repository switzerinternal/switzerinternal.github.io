Info

We print and sell limited editions of paintings and photographs from a small studio in Geneva.

Every print is made to order on cotton paper, checked by the artist, then signed and numbered. Editions are small and never reprinted once they sell out.

We work with a few artists at a time and pay them a fair share of every sale. Framing is done by hand, two streets away.
