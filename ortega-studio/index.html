[ Intro ]

Rafa Ortega is a photographer and filmmaker working out of a small studio in Almería. Twelve years of editorials, campaigns and short films — mostly made in hard southern light, always with a small crew.

[ Approach ]

Long scouts, few setups, no shot list longer than a napkin. The pictures should feel found rather than built — for print, for screens, and for everything in between.
