About me.

I grew up between Budapest and Vienna, studied graphic design at the Angewandte and spent my first years in a small editorial studio, learning that restraint is a skill. Today I lead projects end to end, concept, casting, shoot and layout, with a tight circle of photographers, stylists and type designers across Europe.
